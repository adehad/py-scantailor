5. Margins
##########

.. screenshot:: stage5_margins
   :alt: Margins with its Margins and Alignment panels

Sets the white space around the content. **Top**, **Bottom**, **Left** and
**Right** are the margins; the link buttons keep opposite sides equal. Under
**Alignment**, **Match size with other pages** gives every page the same size and
the buttons place the content inside it. **Apply To ...** copies the settings to
other pages.
