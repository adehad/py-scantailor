Export PDF
##########

.. screenshot:: export_pdf
   :alt: The Export PDF dialog

**File > Export PDF ...** bundles the processed pages into one PDF. Pages that are
not processed yet are offered for processing first.

Black and white pages
   The codec for black and white pages, **JBIG2** or **CCITT G4**.

JPEG quality
   Quality of grey and colour pages, 1 to 100.

Carry text layer
   Keeps the text layer of a source PDF. Available only when the project's pages
   came from a PDF.

Output file
   Where the PDF is written. **Browse...** picks another place.
